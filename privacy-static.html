Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

At Chromolog Technologies, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your personal data when you visit our website or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our contact/demo forms or contact us directly. This includes:

  • Personal Details: Name, organization name, and role.
  • Contact Information: Email address and phone number (if provided via chat or phone links).
  • Inquiry Details: The service requested and messages describing your project requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, schedule demos, and provide consultation.
  • To manage and execute software development contracts and services.
  • To send you updates regarding your project, support queries, or official communications.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your security is important to us. We store your contact details using secure third-party platforms (such as Formspree for forms processing) and our encrypted databases. We implement robust technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share data only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering services (such as mail servers or platform hosting providers), provided they agree to keep this information confidential and secure.

5. Your Rights (DPDPA 2023 Compliance)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and other global privacy frameworks, you have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Correction: You can request details of the personal data we hold and ask us to correct any inaccuracies.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the details provided below.
